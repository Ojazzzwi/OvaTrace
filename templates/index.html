Dispersion Metrics & Covariance Analysis

Dispersion

Correlation matrix

🔍 Key Concepts

  • Variance: how spread out data is from the mean
  • Std Dev: square root of variance, same units as data
  • Covariance: joint variability between feature pairs
  • Correlation: normalized covariance, bounded to [-1, 1]

Distribution Shape: Skewness & Kurtosis

📊 Distribution Analysis

  • Skewness: asymmetry of distribution
  • Symmetric: skewness ≈ 0
  • Right-skewed: positive skewness
  • Left-skewed: negative skewness
  • Kurtosis: tail heaviness and peakedness

      PCOS rate by fast food & exercise

      PCOS rate by BMI band & exercise

      Read carefully: fast-food intake shows a strong association with PCOS (about 50% vs 11–26%). Exercise does not look protective here, and exercisers with BMI ≥30 are only n=4. This is likely because patients already diagnosed or symptomatic are more likely to start exercising (reverse causation) and because exercise is a single self-reported yes/no. This data cannot show that exercise does or doesn't help.
